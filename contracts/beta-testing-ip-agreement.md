# Beta Testing and Intellectual Property Agreement

This Agreement is entered into as of _______________, by and between Joshua Hickman ("Developer") and Zoch Brewing Company, LLC, a Texas limited liability company doing business as Gidding Brewhaus, located at 199 North Burleson Street, Giddings, Texas 78942, including its staff, employees, and owners, collectively "Party Two, the Company."

## Section 1. Purpose

Developer has created a proprietary automated keg washing and brewery inventory management system (the "Product"), including control software, hardware integration, remote monitoring dashboards, and an SI-assisted inventory interface. For purposes of this Agreement, "Super Intelligence" or "SI" means the technologies and systems encompassed by "artificial intelligence" as defined in section 9401(3) of title 15, United States Code, consistent with Executive Order 14434. Where a specific technical component is named, the term "AI" may be used to refer to that component.

## Section 2. Grant of Testing Rights

The Company grants Developer a non-exclusive, non-transferable, royalty-free license to install, operate, and beta-test the Product at the Company's location solely for development, refinement, and validation. This grant conveys no ownership interest in the Product to the Company.

## Section 3. Intellectual Property Ownership

All right, title, and interest in and to the Product — including all software, source code, hardware designs, documentation, trade secrets, and any improvements or modifications made during testing — remain the sole and exclusive property of Developer. The Company acquires no rights by permitting testing, providing feedback, or contributing mechanical or operational knowledge. Any suggestions or input from the Company may be incorporated at Developer's sole discretion without creating an ownership claim.

## Section 4. Company Obligations

The Company shall provide reasonable access to premises, necessary utilities, and operational context during agreed testing hours. The Company shall not reverse-engineer, copy, distribute, or commercially exploit the Product without Developer's prior written consent. This obligation binds the Company as an entity, so any act by staff, employees, or owners in violation is attributable to the Company.

## Section 5. Data Ownership

All test data, logs, fermentation records, and inventory records generated during beta testing remain the property of Developer, except that the Company retains ownership of its underlying business data as it existed before testing.

## Section 6. No Compensation for Testing

This Agreement does not obligate Developer to provide the Product at any particular price or timeline. Any commercial arrangement shall be negotiated separately in a future written agreement.

## Section 7. Independent Contractor Status

Nothing in this Agreement creates employment, partnership, or joint venture. Developer is an independent contractor.

## Section 8. Term and Termination

This Agreement begins on the effective date and continues until terminated by either Party with thirty (30) days' written notice. Upon termination, Developer shall remove the Product within a reasonable time, and all IP and data provisions survive.

## Section 9. Limitation of Liability

The Product is provided as-is for testing. The Company acknowledges the experimental nature and assumes reasonable operational risk. Developer shall not be liable for incidental damages except in cases of gross negligence or willful misconduct.

## Section 10. Decline of Purchase Option

If the Company declines or fails to exercise the purchase option under the separate At-Cost Purchase Option Agreement, Developer is free to sell, license, or otherwise commercially exploit the Product to any other party without restriction, and the Company shall have no claim to exclusivity, first refusal, or any other commercial right.

## Section 11. Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Texas.

## Section 12. Entire Agreement

This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the Parties regarding testing and IP ownership and supersedes all prior oral or written agreements on this subject.
